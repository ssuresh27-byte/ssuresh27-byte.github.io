Meditation

I think meditation is a very useful thing. We can think of ourselves as a CPU. Think about the task manager: sometimes there are background tasks sucking up memory and battery, and we want to reduce those meaningless background tasks as much as possible. Meditation is almost like clearing your task manager completely. And our happiness seems pretty connected to our task-manager status — the more unnecessary, worrying thoughts we have, the more unhappy we tend to be.
