How to be the best at what you do

The people who are best at what they do often aren’t worried about competition with each other. They have goals that extend far beyond being better than another human — something like aiming for “perfection,” or making a big breakthrough. (In a sport it’s a little different, since part of being good is being able to predict your opponent.)
