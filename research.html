SeQUeNCe.DQC: A Full-Stack Simulator for Distributed Quantum Computing

Under review · IEEE ICC 2027

S. Suresh, C. Zhan, J. Chung, R. Kettimuthu

GitHub →

A full-stack simulator for distributed quantum computing, built on Argonne's SeQUeNCe, that reproduces the 71% distributed-Grover success rate of a Nature trapped-ion experiment.

PyEncode: An Open-Source Library for Structured Quantum State Preparation

Quantum Science and Technology, 2026

K. Suresh, S. Suresh

arXiv →GitHub →

A library that exploits the mathematical structure of scientific vectors to prepare quantum states in poly(m) gates, with no materialization or approximation.

Computing a Sparse Approximate Inverse on Quantum Annealing Machines

S. Suresh, K. Suresh

Quantum-annealing algorithms for sparse approximate inverse preconditioners, mapping inequality-constrained optimization to QUBO problems on D-Wave hardware.

S. Suresh, K. Suresh

A box-contraction method that speeds up linear-system solving by 30–40%, tested on 2D-Poisson, thermal, and topology-optimization problems.
