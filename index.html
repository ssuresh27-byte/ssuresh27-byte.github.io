/ / / Resume / CV

Hi, I’m Sanjay, a graduate student at UW–Madison in the MS in Physics (Quantum Computing) program. Currently I’m part of the QUEST lab at UW–Madison, where I work on compilers for modular, fault-tolerant architectures with Prof. Swamit Tannu, and I also collaborate with Dr. Sathya Subramanian at Oxford on quantum-complexity theory. Previously, I worked at Argonne National Lab, where I designed and built SeQUeNCe.DQC, a full-stack distributed quantum computing simulator. I’ve also worked on efficient statevector preparation, fault-tolerant benchmarking, and quantum annealing.

Alongside research, I build tools to investigate how both research and learning can be accelerated by LLMs. One such project is Studia, an open-source framework that lets professors design lessons an AI tutor executes. It’s currently being used by Prof. Swamit Tannu in his course on Systems Architecture for Quantum Computing (CS 639). If you’re interested in using Studia for your class, please contact me at ssuresh27@wisc.edu.

On the side, I pursue Carnatic violin professionally. Please contact me at sanjay.carnatic@gmail.com for any music-related inquiries.
