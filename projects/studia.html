Studia

AI-powered checkpoint-based tutoring platform

Studia is a full-stack AI tutoring platform. Educators build structured lessons out of checkpoints, and students work through them in conversation with an AI tutor that guides them toward understanding rather than just handing over answers. It pulls context from course PDFs through retrieval (RAG), generates performance-based reviews from how a student did, and renders concepts on the fly with real-time Manim animations.

It is currently deployed in CS 639 (Systems Architecture for Quantum Computing) at UW–Madison, taught by Prof. Swamit Tannu, where course material is turned into interactive, tutor-led lessons that students work through at their own pace.
