SurfaceScope

Interactive visualizer for quantum error correction

An interactive web-based visualizer for surface-code quantum error correction. Step-by-step exploration of noise injection, syndrome extraction, detector error models, and minimum-weight perfect matching decoding. Includes a multi-qubit lattice surgery pipeline compiling OpenQASM circuits through native gate decomposition, qubit routing with SWAP insertion, and surgery scheduling with per-slot error correction simulation.

Python · Flask · React · TypeScript · Stim · PyMatching
